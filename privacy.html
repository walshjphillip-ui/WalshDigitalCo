Privacy

What this site knows about you.

Very little, and only what you hand over. There are no accounts here, no tracking pixels and no advertising. This page says plainly what does happen.

06

Last updated 17 August 2026

Who runs this site

Walsh Digital Co., an independent studio operated by Phillip Walsh. For anything on this page, write to hello@walshdigitalco.com.

What the site collects directly

Only what you type into the contact form. There are no sign-ups, no logins and no shopping cart. The site sets no cookies of its own, and there is no advertising or session-recording software on any page. The plan finder and the package builder run entirely in your browser — the choices you make are never sent anywhere, and they are gone the moment you close the tab.

What happens anyway

Two things are worth naming, because they are true of this site even though the studio does not collect anything itself.

Typefaces are loaded from Google. The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers. Google can see your IP address and basic browser information as part of that request. This is a consequence of how the fonts are served, not something the studio receives a copy of. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The host keeps server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps ordinary request logs — IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time — for security and to keep the service running. That is standard for any website, including ones with no tracking at all.

The contact form

The plans page has a form. What you type into it is sent to the studio so it can be replied to, and it is used for nothing else — no list, no advertising, no sharing. If you would rather not use it, every phone and email link on the site reaches the same place.

If you email the studio

Every contact link on this site opens your own mail app. If you write in, your message and address sit in the studio's mailbox and are used to reply to you and to carry on the conversation. They are not added to a mailing list, and they are not sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing.

If you become a client

Client work is separate from this website. Running a client's site, search presence or social accounts can involve handling information on their behalf — that is governed by the written agreement for the work, not by this page.

Your choices

Because the site holds nothing about you, there is nothing here to export or delete. If you have emailed the studio and want that correspondence removed, ask and it will be deleted. Any question about this page can go to the same address.

Children

This site sells services to businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If any of the above stops being true — if a contact form or analytics is ever added — this page gets updated before it ships, and the date at the top changes with it.
